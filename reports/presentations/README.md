## Interpretation

Completed trips measure served demand. Pricing and credit comparisons are observational. Credit contribution, redemption and incremental-trip cost are scenario assumptions. Forecast scores are retrospective and remain separate from the original cutoff memo. The credit test is a proposal that needs internal user and redemption data. The Power BI model is a source draft; native DAX, refresh, filter behavior and PBIX validation remain pending.
